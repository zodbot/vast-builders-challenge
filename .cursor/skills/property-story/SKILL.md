name: property-story
description: Produce a lively Property Studio realtor video script and image storyboard from reviewed evidence and explicit financial scenarios.
---

From `tools/property-studio`, run `python -m property_studio script RUN_ID`. W&B inference supplies generated scripts when configured; otherwise a clearly labeled template draft is returned. Review factual claims first in the app.

Start with an interesting question, show the actual appeal, introduce a sourced complication, and pay off the question with a conditional verdict. Keep jokes and energy; preserve material uncertainty in spoken words. Missing records do not establish wrongdoing. Do not promise virality or investment returns.

Every factual scene needs claim IDs, numeric scenes need calculation IDs, and photos need authorized media IDs. Scene durations are editorial estimates. Review hooks, captions and DM drafts as carefully as narration. Generate drafts only; do not post or send replies. Video rendering is a later integration.
